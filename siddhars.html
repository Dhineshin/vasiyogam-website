பதினெண் சித்தர்கள்The 18 Siddhars

The mystic saints of the Tamil tradition — masters of yoga, medicine, alchemy, and poetry — who made the breath the doorway to liberation.

Divine Leader

முருகன்Lord Murugan

Lord Murugan — the eternal youth, bearer of the vel (the spear of wisdom) and rider of the peacock — is revered as the divine guru and leader of the 18 Siddhars. The Siddhar path is inseparable from His grace.

The bond is a living one: Siddhar Bogar himself fashioned the famed navapashana (nine-herb mineral) idol of Murugan at Palani, worshipped to this day. To follow the Siddhars is to walk in Murugan's light.

The Eighteen

The saints below follow the commonly cited traditional list of the பதினெண் சித்தர்கள் — the eighteen Siddhars of Tamil tradition.

1

Nandhi Devar நந்தி தேவர்

Sivagnana · Yoga

Foremost disciple of Lord Shiva and guru of Thirumoolar; master of Sivagnana, the wisdom of Shiva.

2

Agathiyar அகத்தியர்

Medicine · Tamil grammar

Father of the Tamil language and of Siddha medicine; his works span medicine, alchemy, and yoga, and he initiated disciples including Bogar.

3

Thirumoolar திருமூலர்

Yoga · Literature

Author of the Thirumandiram — 3,000 verses on yoga, tantra, and Saiva philosophy, a cornerstone of the tradition.

4

Bogar போகர்

Alchemy · Medicine

Master of alchemy (rasavada); fashioned the navapashana Murugan idol at Palani, a wonder of Siddhar science.

5

Konganar கோங்கணர்

Alchemy · Medicine

Alchemist and physician of Bogar's lineage; wrote extensively on transmutation and Siddha pharmacology.

6

Machamuni மச்சமுனி

Yoga · Breath

Yogi and master of pranayama; disciple of Nandhi Devar, carrying the breath-yoga at the heart of Vaasi Yogam.

7

Korakkar கோரக்கர்

Yoga · Medicine

Adept of hatha yoga and Siddha medicine; his works bridge yogic discipline and the healing arts.

8

Sattamuni சட்டைமுனி

Alchemy · Medicine

Alchemist and physician; tradition holds he attained samadhi at Srirangam after a life of sadhana.

9

Sundaranandar சுந்தரானந்தர்

Alchemy · Yoga

Alchemist-yogi of the Bogar lineage; master of both the laboratory and the inner fire of yoga.

10

Ramadevar இராமதேவர்

Alchemy · Medicine

Master of alchemy and Siddha medicine; his treatises detail the preparation of potent herbo-mineral remedies.

11

Kudambai குடம்பை சித்தர்

Literature · Philosophy

Mystic poet; his Kudambai songs carry profound Vedantic wisdom in simple, piercing Tamil.

12

Karuvoorar கருவூரார்

Temple arts · Sculpture

Master of temple architecture and sacred sculpture; consecrated temple idols, uniting art with spiritual power.

13

Idaikadar இடைக்காடர்

Astronomy · Medicine

Astronomer-astrologer and physician of the Siddha tradition, famed for his knowledge of celestial cycles.

14

Kamalamuni கமலமுனி

Alchemy · Yoga

Alchemist and yogi; his works explore the refinement of metals as a mirror of the refinement of the self.

15

Vanmigar வான்மீகர்

Literature · Medicine

Sage-poet honored among the eighteen; in Siddhar tradition he is remembered for wisdom spanning poetry and healing.

16

Pathanjali பதஞ்சலி

Yoga · Philosophy

Author of the Yoga Sutras; honored in the Siddha tradition, which holds his samadhi at Rameshwaram.

17

Dhanvanthri தன்வந்திரி

Medicine

The divine physician; foundational works on Siddha medicine are attributed to him, with samadhi at Vaitheeswaran Koil.

18

Pambatti பாம்பாட்டி சித்தர்

Literature · Medicine

Mystic poet renowned for his songs and for deep knowledge of venoms and their antidotes.

A note on the list: different Tamil sources name the eighteen slightly differently — some include figures like Kagapujandar, Pulippani, or Theraiyar among the Siddhars. This page follows the most commonly cited traditional list. All eighteen are honored equally as masters of the path.

Their Path Is the Breath

The Siddhars' greatest gift is Vaasi Yogam itself — the sadhana of the breath they practiced and perfected.
